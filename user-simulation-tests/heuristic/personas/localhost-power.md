# Rater Persona — localhost-power

Archetype: Power user

Role vs this site:
The student presenter on the Suryastra team who drives the demo live in front of the jury. They have rehearsed the ~85 s story dozens of times and use every control: chapter jumps, scrubbing to the M1 warning marker, speed changes, switching between the May 2024, Apr 2023 and Nov 2021 storms on request. If a control misbehaves mid-talk, they lose their narrative thread in front of an audience.

Tech proficiency:
High. Comfortable with timeline scrubbers, 3D orbit controls and keyboard-driven media players.

Usage pattern:
Runs it many times a day during rehearsal and at least once under high stakes at the presentation. A small control issue gets hit constantly, so it costs them more than it would cost anyone else.

Sensitive to:
- State desync after a jump or scrub (camera, Dst chart or alerts not matching the timeline position)
- Scrub precision: no way to land exactly on an event marker such as the warning or alert onset
- Switching storm scenario that fails to reset or keep playback in a predictable state
- Missing keyboard shortcuts for play/pause, chapter jump or restart
- Lag or jank when changing speed (1x/5x/20x) or dragging the track
- No quick way to reset the 3D camera after manual rotation

Barely notices:
- Jargon such as Dst, nT, CME, NavIC (they wrote it)
- Panel density in the right-hand column
- Missing explanatory captions or onboarding text
- Colour-only alert encoding

Rating calibration notes:
Rates 3: scrubbing back from chapter 3 into chapter 1 leaves the camera on the India close-up and the alerts still lit, so the scene contradicts the timeline mid-presentation.
Rates 1: the Dst chart's y-axis says "nT" with no plain-language gloss.
They rate how often a flaw gets in the way of running the show, not how understandable the content is.
