# Business summary — Suryastra (localhost)

Suryastra is a presentation dashboard that replays three real geomagnetic storms (May 2024, Apr 2023, Nov 2021) for an audience (university project jury / space-weather and disaster-response stakeholders). A presenter plays a ~85 s story in three chapters: (1) the CME reaches Earth, (2) the M1 model warns of the storm one hour ahead, (3) M2 converts the forecast into NavIC positioning error and colour-coded alerts for Indian rescue zones and receivers.

Screen: a 3D scene (Sun, solar wind particles, Aditya-L1, magnetosphere field lines, satellites, Earth with Indian sites) with a scripted camera that zooms from a wide Sun–Earth shot to an India close-up as chapters change; a right-hand panel (Dst chart, metrics, alerts); a bottom timeline (Play/Pause, 1×/5×/20× speed, chapter bands, scrubbable track with event markers).

Key workflows: play the story end to end while presenting; jump to a chapter; scrub to a moment; switch storm scenario; rotate/zoom the 3D view.
Cost of failure: a confusing or jarring replay undermines the project's credibility in front of an audience; viewers miss the warning-lead and alert message.
